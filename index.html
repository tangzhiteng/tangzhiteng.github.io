About Me

I am a direct-entry Ph.D. student at Wuhan University of Technology, advised by Prof. Junwei Zhou . My research focuses on task-oriented and generative image compression, diffusion models, and multi-task visual perception. In particular, I explore how to preserve and enhance task-relevant information under extremely low bitrates to support object detection, instance segmentation, and semantic segmentation.

Research Interests: Task-oriented Image Compression · Generative Image Compression · Diffusion Models · Multi-task Visual Perception
